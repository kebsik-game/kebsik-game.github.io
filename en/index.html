Kebsik: Kebab Shop Simulator

Kebsik is a kebab shop simulator and restaurant tycoon game for Android. Open a small kebab stand: set prices, order ingredients, hire your crew and grow the shop. Choose where to invest the money you earn.

The Android release is being prepared. Google Play launch will follow testing.

Your shop, your decisions

Match your prices to your quality, watch your stock and find out why customers leave the queue. A bigger grill helps when demand grows; an extra worker brings both benefits and costs. Monthly reports show your revenue, expenses and result.

A goal at every stage

Complete tasks to earn experience and rewards. Unlock new options, expand your menu and plan your next investment. Pan Mistrz helps you learn life behind the counter, while short activities let you care for guests and attract new customers.

A living town

Weather, events and decision cards affect your business. Customers arrive, place orders and sit at tables. Pause time, think through your decision and return to running the shop.

Play your way

Choose a difficulty. Develop the business through prices, quality, menu, staff and marketing. This is a business game about restaurant management: decisions have costs and consequences.

Good to know

  • Core gameplay works offline. Purchases, ads, update checks and optional feedback use the internet.
  • No in-game account is required. Your save stays on your device; we recommend using the backup feature.

  • The ad-removal purchase turns off ads between months. Rewarded videos remain optional.
  • Languages: Polish, Turkish, English and German.

Questions about the game

What is Kebsik?
Kebsik is a kebab shop simulator and restaurant tycoon game for Android. Run a shop in a Polish town: set prices, order ingredients, hire staff and expand the menu.
Does Kebsik work offline?
Core gameplay works offline. Purchases, ads, update and settings checks, and optional feedback use the internet.
Is this a timed cooking game?
It is a restaurant management game. Your results depend on prices, quality, stock, staff and costs. You can pause or speed up time.
How does the shop progress?
Complete tasks to earn experience and rewards. Levels unlock investments, a bigger meat cone, extra grills and options for expanding the menu.
Do I need an account?
No in-game account is required. Your save stays on the device; use the backup feature.
Are there ads and purchases?
The game includes ads and optional one-time purchases. There is no subscription or loot box system. The ad-removal purchase turns off ads between months; rewarded videos remain optional.
Which languages are supported?
Kebsik supports Polish, English, German and Turkish.
Where can I download Kebsik?
The Android version is being prepared for Google Play. A download link will be added after testing and confirmed publication.
