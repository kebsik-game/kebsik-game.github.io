Kebsik Mini privacy policy

Kebsik Mini · Android · 2026-11-05

Local game data

Progress, player profiles, language and sound preferences are stored on your device. No Kebsik account or developer-operated game backend is required. The app does not upload your save to the developer. Exporting a save creates a JSON document in the location you choose; protect that file if you share your device.

Optional advertising

Optional reward videos use Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform. Core play is available without watching ads. We refresh consent information each session and use the provider's current eligibility result before requesting an ad. Ad choices are available from the game's settings when Google requires them. Refusing personalized ads does not mean that all provider data processing stops.

Data processed by Google

Google's advertising SDK describes collection and sharing of IP addresses, interactions, diagnostics, advertising/app-set IDs and applicable device or account identifiers for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. IP addresses may indicate approximate location. Google states that SDK data is encrypted in transit using TLS. Android advertising ID controls and applicable consent settings affect processing. See Google's linked privacy information.

Optional purchases

Optional one-time purchases use Google Play Billing. Google handles payment details; the game does not request your card number. The app verifies store-signed purchase information and keeps a separate local entitlement record with transaction hashes rather than a stored raw receipt or purchase token. Restoring and reconciling purchases requires the appropriate Play account and a connection. Store accounts, payment history and refunds are governed by Google Play.

Your controls and retention

You can change local preferences and export your save in Settings. Android's clear-app-data or uninstall controls remove local app data; retain an exported save first if you want to keep progress. These actions do not automatically cancel a store purchase, erase Google's records or delete an exported document. Advertising IDs are controlled in Android settings. Contact us about privacy requests; only information actually held by the developer can be addressed by the developer.

Audience and changes

The game is intended for adults. We do not ask players for birth dates, contacts, precise GPS location, microphone or camera access. This policy will be updated when the app’s data practices change; the date above identifies this version.

Privacy rights and requests

Where applicable, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability, object to processing based on legitimate interests and withdraw consent. Withdrawal does not affect earlier lawful processing. Use Google’s in-game ad privacy options for advertising choices and contact the controller below about data held by the developer. You may complain to Poland’s data protection authority (UODO, uodo.gov.pl) or your local supervisory authority.

Support and legal bases

If you contact support, we use the information you choose to send to answer your request; do not send passwords, full saves or payment tokens. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to handle the request and any applicable legal obligations. Handling purchase entitlements supports performance of the purchase contract; support and app security rely on legitimate interests, while advertising consent is handled through Google’s consent framework where required. Google and the support email provider may process data outside your country, including in the United States, subject to their privacy notices and applicable transfer safeguards.
