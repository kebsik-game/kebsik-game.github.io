Test Kebsik on Android

Kebsik — a mobile game about running a kebab shop, now in pre-release testing.

Android only. iPhone and iPad users cannot join this test.

1. Join the Google Group2. Become a tester and download

Sign in to Google and use the same account in the group, on the opt-in page and in the Play Store. Your email is hidden from other testers by the group settings.

Please stay opted into the test for 14 days, keep the game installed and open it occasionally to play. Try ordinary tasks and tell us what is unclear or needs fixing.

Please do not try purchases with real money. Cancel if the payment screen does not explicitly show a test card and no charge.

Send feedback through the in-game form or email kayacidev@gmail.com.

Cannot join? Check that the same Google account is selected in the group, on the opt-in page and in the Play Store. If the game is not visible yet, refresh the store and try again.
