Help test Kebsik

Kebsik — a mobile game about running a kebab shop, now in testing before launch.

Android only. iPhone and iPad users cannot join this test.

Sign-ups are not open yet. Please return when the links are ready.

  1. 1. Join the group

    Join the group. Sign in with the Google account you use on Google Play and join the group.

  2. 2. Become a tester and download

    Become a tester and download. On the Play page, choose “Become a tester”, then download the game from Google Play. Use the same account for both steps.

You do not need to send us your email. Joining the group requires signing in. Other testers cannot see your email, the member list or messages; only group administrators can access them. Google knows which account you use.

Stay with us for 14 days

For at least 14 days, please stay opted in, remain in the group and keep the game installed. Open it from time to time, actually play, and tell us what works and what needs improvement.

Please do not try purchases with real money

Please do not try purchases with real money. No purchase is needed to take part. Only eligible license tester accounts have Google Play test payment methods that do not charge money. If you see a regular card or no test purchase notice, cancel.

Share your feedback

Use the feedback form in the game: Menu → About → Report a problem / suggestion, or write to kayacidev@gmail.com.

For a bug, include the steps, phone model, Android version and game version. Do not send payment details or passwords.

Cannot download?

Check that you joined the group and opted in with the account used on Play. If you joined an internal test before, you may need to leave it first. Access can take a little time after joining.
