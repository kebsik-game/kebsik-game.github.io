Kebsik — Privacy Policy / Polityka prywatności / Gizlilik politikası / Datenschutzerklärung

Last updated: 2026-10-17

English

Kebsik is a game that works offline. It has no game accounts and no independent gameplay analytics. Advertising-provider measurement is described below. It shows short full-screen ads between in-game months, which you can remove with the one-time "Remove ads" purchase, and optional rewarded videos you choose to watch. Kebsik is not directed at children under 13.

What stays on your device. Your save game, shop name, owner names and settings, a local copy of your unlocked purchases, and a short local log of the last app errors (for support). The game itself never sends this data anywhere. Uninstalling the game deletes it. If Android backup is turned on on your device, Android may include this app data in the device backup kept in your Google account and restore it on a new phone; the developer has no access to these backups, and you can turn backup off in your device settings.

Purchases. In-app purchases are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play and processed through RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA, revenuecat.com) to verify receipts and restore purchases. When the game starts it asks RevenueCat which items you own; this happens for every player, also if you have never bought anything. RevenueCat receives a pseudonymous app user ID (a random identifier created on your device; it is not your name, email or advertising ID), your purchase records and technical request data such as the IP address — not your name, email or card details. You can see this ID under Menu → About → Support ID. RevenueCat keeps the records for as long as they are needed to restore your purchases and for the developer's legal record-keeping duties, or until you ask for deletion. See https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

Ads. The game shows optional rewarded videos and occasional full-screen ads at natural breaks. Full-screen ads appear only after you close a monthly report, never during the tutorial or the first 6 in-game months, and only after at least 15 minutes of active play in the session. At least 15 minutes and 3 monthly reports separate them; none appear within 3 minutes of a rewarded video. There are at most 2 per session and 3 in any rolling 24 hours. If no ad is ready, the game continues without waiting. The one-time "Remove ads" purchase (also included in the "Upgrade bundle") permanently removes these full-screen ads. Rewarded videos are optional: choosing the sponsor option can grant an in-game bonus, at most once per in-game month. No banner ads or automatic cash-shortage offer pop-ups are shown. Ads are served by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Ltd.). All players receive restricted, non-personalised ads: every consent-information request is tagged as under the age of consent and every ad request asks for non-personalised ads, without asking for a date of birth. These settings disable personalisation and remarketing and prevent transmission of the Android advertising ID. Google may still process IP addresses, approximate location derived from them, device and diagnostic data, and ad interactions to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. Before requesting ads, the game checks Google's consent framework; if ads are not permitted, none are loaded. With this restricted setting Google does not request personalised-ad consent. If Google requires privacy options, they are available under Menu → About → Ad privacy settings. See https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Game updates. In the Android and iOS app, each time the game starts it asks the update server of Expo (650 Industries, Inc., USA; expo.dev) whether a newer version of the game code is available, and downloads it if so. The request contains a random installation ID created by the update component on your device (not your advertising ID, not linked to your name or purchases), the app version, update channel and platform and, like any web request, your IP address. It is used only to deliver fixes and updates and to show the developer aggregate launch and error counts per update; it is not used for ads or tracking and not shared with anyone else. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data removes the ID. See https://expo.dev/privacy.

Game settings file. When the app starts it downloads a small public settings file from the developer's website (kebsik-game.github.io, hosted by GitHub, Inc.) that can adjust ad frequency or show a short notice. No personal data is sent; like any web request, GitHub receives your IP address and technical request data under its own privacy statement (https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

Error reports (internal test builds only). Only internal test builds that the developer installs on its own test devices send error reports to Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.); the versions on Google Play and the App Store and the web game never do. These reports contain technical JavaScript error messages and stack traces, the app version and platform; Sentry also sees the IP address used for delivery. No save games, shop or owner names, feedback messages, purchase records, screenshots, session recordings, device identifiers or native crash data are sent. See https://sentry.io/privacy/.

Feedback form (optional). Under Menu → About → Report a problem / suggestion you can write to the developer. Your name is optional; an e-mail address is enough for a reply. Only if you fill in the form and tick the consent box, your e-mail address, message, your name if you entered one and a few technical details (app version, device platform and system version, game language, currency and shop level; never your save game) are sent to the developer, either through the e-mail forwarding service Web3Forms (web3forms.com, see https://web3forms.com/privacy), which passes the message on to the developer's mailbox, or through your own e-mail app. They are used only to answer you, are kept for up to 12 months and then deleted.

Permissions. The game asks for no access to photos, camera, microphone, location or contacts. AdMob is configured not to transmit the Android advertising ID; the technical ad data described above may still be processed. The "Share" button on an event card only opens your device's own share menu; the game itself sends nothing.

Legal bases (GDPR Art. 6). Purchase checks and restoring purchases: performance of the purchase contract (Art. 6(1)(b)); for players who have not bought anything, our legitimate interest in reliably restoring purchases (Art. 6(1)(f)). Non-personalised ads: our legitimate interest in financing a free game (Art. 6(1)(f)). Personalised ads are not requested. Storage or access requiring consent remains subject to the applicable consent requirements; no ad is loaded unless Google's consent framework permits requests. Game updates, the game settings file and error reports in internal test builds: our legitimate interest in delivering fixes and running the game securely (Art. 6(1)(f)). Feedback messages: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)).

Your rights. Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  1. access your data and get a copy (Art. 15);
  2. have incorrect data corrected (Art. 16);
  3. have your data deleted (Art. 17);
  4. restrict processing (Art. 18);
  5. data portability (Art. 20);
  6. object at any time to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21);
  7. withdraw your consent at any time; this does not affect processing that took place before the withdrawal (Art. 7(3));
  8. lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — in Poland the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO, uodo.gov.pl), or the authority of the country where you live or work.

How to make a request (including deletion). E-mail kayacidev@gmail.com and include your Support ID from Menu → About → Support ID; without it your pseudonymous purchase records cannot be found. The developer answers within one month and, for a deletion request, deletes your customer record at RevenueCat and any feedback messages from you. Deleting the RevenueCat record does not remove your purchases from your store account; you can get them back later with "Restore purchases". Everything stored on your device is deleted when you uninstall the game. Ad data is controlled by Google. Where Google requires privacy options, use Menu → About → Ad privacy settings; you can also reset or delete the advertising ID in your device settings. The update installation ID is random and not linked to you; uninstalling the game removes it.

Children. Kebsik is not directed at children under 13, and its store listing is for players aged 13 and over. Playing does not require your name or e-mail; the optional feedback form can include them. If you believe a child under 13 has sent us personal data, write to kayacidev@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Contact. Data controller: Hüseyin Kayacı, e-mail: kayacidev@gmail.com.

Transfers outside the EU. RevenueCat, Inc., Google LLC (ads), 650 Industries, Inc. (Expo, game updates) and GitHub, Inc. (settings file) are based in the USA. Transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. Feedback messages may be processed outside the EU by Web3Forms and by the developer's e-mail provider (Google). Error reports from internal test builds are stored in Sentry's EU region.

Changes. If this policy changes, the date at the top is updated and the new version is shown in the game. Material changes, such as a new type of data or a new recipient, are announced in the game before they take effect.

Reklamy. Gra pokazuje dobrowolne filmy z nagrodą i sporadyczne reklamy pełnoekranowe w naturalnych przerwach. Reklama pełnoekranowa może pojawić się tylko po zamknięciu raportu miesięcznego, nigdy podczas samouczka ani w pierwszych 6 miesiącach gry, i dopiero po co najmniej 15 minutach aktywnej gry w sesji. Między reklamami musi upłynąć co najmniej 15 minut i 3 raporty miesięczne; przez 3 minuty po filmie z nagrodą nie ma takiej reklamy. Limit wynosi 2 na sesję i 3 w dowolnych kolejnych 24 godzinach. Jeśli reklama nie jest gotowa, gra nie czeka. Jednorazowy zakup „Usuń reklamy” (także w „Pakiecie ulepszeń”) usuwa te reklamy na stałe. Filmy z nagrodą są dobrowolne: wybranie opcji sponsora może dać bonus w grze, najwyżej raz na miesiąc gry. Nie ma banerów ani automatycznych propozycji wyświetlanych przy braku pieniędzy. Reklamy dostarcza Google AdMob (Google Ireland Ltd.). Wszyscy gracze otrzymują ograniczone, niespersonalizowane reklamy: gra ustawia znacznik Google dla osób poniżej wieku samodzielnego wyrażania zgody i przy każdym żądaniu wybiera reklamy niespersonalizowane, bez pytania o datę urodzenia. Ustawienia wyłączają personalizację i remarketing oraz zapobiegają przesyłaniu identyfikatora reklamowego Androida. Google może nadal przetwarzać adres IP, wynikającą z niego przybliżoną lokalizację, dane urządzenia i diagnostyczne oraz interakcje z reklamą, aby wyświetlać i mierzyć reklamy oraz zapobiegać nadużyciom. Przed pobraniem reklamy gra sprawdza wynik mechanizmu zgody Google; jeśli reklamy nie są dozwolone, nie pobiera ich. W tym ograniczonym trybie Google nie prosi o zgodę na reklamy spersonalizowane. Jeśli Google wymaga opcji prywatności, są one dostępne w Menu → O grze → Ustawienia prywatności reklam. Zob. https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Türkçe

Kebsik çevrimdışı çalışan bir oyundur. Oyun hesabı ve bağımsız oynanış analitiği yoktur. Reklam sağlayıcısının ölçümleri aşağıda açıklanmıştır. Oyun aylar arasında kısa tam ekran reklamlar gösterir (tek seferlik "Reklamları kaldır" alımıyla kaldırabilirsin); bir de senin izlemeyi seçtiğin isteğe bağlı ödüllü videolar vardır. Kebsik 13 yaşından küçük çocuklara yönelik değildir.

Cihazında kalanlar. Kayıt, dükkân adı, sahip adları ve ayarlar, açtığın satın almaların yerel bir kopyası ve son uygulama hatalarının kısa bir yerel kaydı (destek için). Oyunun kendisi bu verileri hiçbir yere göndermez. Oyunu silmek onları da siler. Cihazında Android yedeklemesi açıksa Android bu uygulama verilerini Google hesabındaki cihaz yedeğine ekleyebilir ve yeni telefonda geri yükleyebilir; geliştiricinin bu yedeklere erişimi yoktur, yedeklemeyi cihaz ayarlarından kapatabilirsin.

Satın almalar. Uygulama içi satın almaları Apple App Store ya da Google Play yürütür; RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., ABD, revenuecat.com) makbuzları doğrular ve alımları geri yükler. Oyun açılırken hangi ürünlere sahip olduğunu RevenueCat'e sorar; bu, hiç satın alma yapmamış olsan da her oyuncuda olur. RevenueCat takma adlı bir uygulama kullanıcı kimliği (cihazında oluşturulan rastgele bir kimlik; adın, e-postan ya da reklam kimliğin değildir), alım kayıtlarını ve IP adresi gibi isteğin teknik bilgilerini alır — adını, e-postanı ya da kart bilgilerini almaz. Bu kimliği Menü → Hakkında → Destek kimliği'nde görebilirsin. RevenueCat kayıtları alımlarını geri yükleyebilmek ve geliştiricinin yasal kayıt tutma yükümlülükleri için gerektiği sürece ya da silinmesini isteyene kadar saklar. Bkz. https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

Reklamlar. Oyun isteğe bağlı ödüllü videolar ve doğal molalarda seyrek tam ekran reklamlar gösterir. Tam ekran reklam yalnız ay raporu kapatılınca, rehber dışında ve ilk 6 oyun ayından sonra; ayrıca oturumda en az 15 dakika etkin oyun süresi geçince gösterilebilir. Aralarında en az 15 dakika ve 3 ay raporu bulunur; ödüllü videodan sonra 3 dakika gösterilmez. Oturum başına en fazla 2, kayan 24 saatte en fazla 3 tam ekran reklam vardır. Reklam hazır değilse oyun bekletilmez. Tek ödemelik „Reklamları kaldır” („Geliştirme paketi”ne de dahil) bu tam ekran reklamları kalıcı olarak kaldırır. Ödüllü video isteğe bağlıdır: sponsor seçeneği oyun içi bonus verebilir, oyun ayında en fazla bir kez. Banner veya para azaldığında kendiliğinden çıkan reklam teklifi yoktur. Reklamları Google AdMob (Google Ireland Ltd.) sunar. Tüm oyunculara sınırlı, kişiselleştirilmemiş reklam istenir: doğum tarihi sorulmadan her oyuncu için Google'ın onay yaşının altındaki kullanıcılara yönelik işareti ve kişiselleştirilmemiş reklam seçeneği uygulanır. Bu ayarlar kişiselleştirme ve yeniden pazarlamayı kapatır, Android reklam kimliğinin iletilmesini önler.
