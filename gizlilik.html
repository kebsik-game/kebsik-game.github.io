Kebsik — Privacy Policy / Polityka prywatności / Gizlilik politikası / Datenschutzerklärung

Last updated: 2026-11-17

English

Kebsik is a game that works offline. It has no game accounts and no independent gameplay analytics. Advertising-provider measurement is described below. It shows short full-screen ads between in-game months, which you can remove with the one-time "Remove ads" purchase, and optional rewarded videos you choose to watch. Kebsik is not directed at children under 13.

What stays on your device. Your save game, shop name, owner names and settings, a local copy of your unlocked purchases, and a short local log of the last app errors (for support). The game itself never sends this data anywhere. Uninstalling the game deletes it. If Android backup is turned on on your device, Android may include this app data in the device backup kept in your Google account and restore it on a new phone; the developer has no access to these backups, and you can turn backup off in your device settings.

Purchases. In-app purchases are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play and processed through RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA, revenuecat.com) to verify receipts and restore purchases. When the game starts it asks RevenueCat which items you own; this happens for every player, also if you have never bought anything. RevenueCat receives a pseudonymous app user ID (a random identifier created on your device; it is not your name, email or advertising ID), your purchase records and technical request data such as the IP address — not your name, email or card details. You can see this ID under Menu → About → Support ID. RevenueCat keeps the records for as long as they are needed to restore your purchases and for the developer's legal record-keeping duties, or until you ask for deletion. See https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

Ads. Android build 82 and later: forced full-screen ads are considered only on entry to a new monthly report, outside the tutorial, after the first 15 cumulative foreground minutes stored on the device and at least 60 seconds after returning to the foreground. A forced ad can appear only at least 10 minutes after any displayed forced or rewarded ad; a displayed rewarded video also restarts this ten-minute protection. There is no additional two-report requirement or daily view-count cap. Optional rewarded videos become available after the tutorial when an ad is ready, with at least 90 seconds between views. Each completed video credits the displayed in-game cash reward; a failed save can be retried without watching again. Earlier Android builds protect the first 15 active minutes per session, consider forced ads when the report closes with a ten-minute/two-report interval and a three-minute post-reward protection, and share a five-minute rewarded interval. In every build, the one-time “Remove ads” purchase (also in the “Upgrade bundle”) permanently removes forced ads while rewarded videos stay optional. No banner ads or automatic cash-shortage offer pop-ups are shown. Ads are served by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Ltd.). All players receive restricted, non-personalised ads: every consent-information request is tagged as under the age of consent and every ad request asks for non-personalised ads, without asking for a date of birth. These settings disable personalisation and remarketing. Where platform permissions allow, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may access the Android advertising ID for advertising delivery, measurement and fraud prevention; the game requests non-personalised ads, not personalised advertising. Google may still process IP addresses, approximate location derived from them, device and diagnostic data, and ad interactions to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. Before requesting ads, the game checks Google's consent framework; if ads are not permitted, none are loaded. With this restricted setting Google does not request personalised-ad consent. If Google requires privacy options, they are available under Menu → About → Ad privacy settings. See https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Game updates. Over-the-air code updates through Expo are disabled in this release. The game does not contact an Expo update server at startup or download game-code updates from it. App updates are delivered through the app store.

Wall posters. Paid wall sponsorship is available only for individually approved campaigns. When no eligible campaign is available, local house posters are displayed. Country and city choices stay on your device; GPS, location permissions and location lookup from your IP address are not used. The shared catalogue and verified images come from the developer's GitHub-hosted website, without country, city or language request parameters. As with any web request, GitHub receives your IP address and technical request data. Posters have no click tracking, impression counter or ad identifier; the separate AdMob processing is described above. Remove ads also hides paid wall sponsors. Ordinary house posters remain visible.

Game settings file. When the app starts it downloads a small public settings file from the developer's website (kebsik-game.github.io, hosted by GitHub, Inc.) that can adjust ad frequency or show a short notice. As with any web request, GitHub receives your IP address and technical request data under its own privacy statement (https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

Error reports (internal test builds only). Only internal test builds that the developer installs on its own test devices send error reports to Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.); the versions on Google Play and the App Store and the web game never do. These reports contain technical JavaScript error messages and stack traces, the app version and platform; Sentry also sees the IP address used for delivery. No save games, shop or owner names, feedback messages, purchase records, screenshots, session recordings, device identifiers or native crash data are sent. See https://sentry.io/privacy/.

Feedback form (optional). In versions with the email option, under Menu → About → Report a problem / suggestion you can prepare a draft in your own email app. Your name is optional. The draft contains your reply address, message and the technical details listed in the form. Your email app handles the draft and sends it when you confirm. Please leave out passwords, card or identity details and game saves. The developer uses feedback to respond and keeps its own correspondence copies for up to 12 months.

Feedback sent through Web3Forms. Some app versions can relay feedback through Web3Forms when the form sends directly. That provider may retain those records for up to three years unless the plan provides a shorter period or the records are deleted earlier. This period is separate from the developer's. See https://web3forms.com/privacy and https://web3forms.com/dpa.

Sponsorship enquiries. The enquiry page prepares an email draft. If you send it, the developer uses your reply address, contact and campaign details and any poster attachment to assess the request, provide a quote or arrange a campaign. The basis is pre-contractual steps you request or, if you contact us on behalf of a business, our legitimate interest in handling that business enquiry. Unsuccessful enquiries are kept for up to 12 months unless needed for a legal claim. Campaign records may be kept longer when required for the contract, accounting or legal claims. Email is handled by the sender’s mail provider and Google for the developer. The rights and contact details below also apply to these enquiries.

Permissions. The game asks for no access to photos, camera, microphone, location or contacts. The advertising build includes permission for the Google Mobile Ads SDK to access the Android advertising ID where available. Non-personalised advertising does not by itself guarantee that no identifiers are processed; the technical ad data described above may also be processed. The "Share" button on an event card only opens your device's own share menu; the game itself sends nothing.

Legal bases (GDPR Art. 6). Purchase checks and restoring purchases: performance of the purchase contract (Art. 6(1)(b)); for players who have not bought anything, our legitimate interest in reliably restoring purchases (Art. 6(1)(f)). Non-personalised ads: our legitimate interest in financing a free game (Art. 6(1)(f)). Personalised ads are not requested. Storage or access requiring consent remains subject to the applicable consent requirements; no ad is loaded unless Google's consent framework permits requests. The game settings file and error reports in internal test builds: our legitimate interest in delivering fixes and running the game securely (Art. 6(1)(f)). Feedback messages: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)).

Your rights. Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  1. access your data and get a copy (Art. 15);
  2. have incorrect data corrected (Art. 16);
  3. have your data deleted (Art. 17);
  4. restrict processing (Art. 18);
  5. data portability (Art. 20);
  6. object at any time to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21);
  7. withdraw your consent at any time; this does not affect processing that took place before the withdrawal (Art. 7(3));
  8. lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — in Poland the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO, uodo.gov.pl), or the authority of the country where you live or work.

How to make a request (including deletion). Email kayacidev@gmail.com to request access, correction or deletion. For purchase records, include the Support ID from Menu → About → Support ID if available. For correspondence, start with the email address you originally used. The developer may request only the information needed to verify the request and normally replies within one month. Deletion may cover eligible correspondence and RevenueCat records, subject to lawful retention requirements. Restoring purchases can recreate a RevenueCat record. Store purchase records and device backups are managed separately by your store or backup provider.

Children. Kebsik is not directed at children under 13, and its store listing is for players aged 13 and over. Playing does not require your name or e-mail; the optional feedback form can include them. If you believe a child under 13 has sent us personal data, write to kayacidev@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Contact. Data controller: Hüseyin Kayacı, e-mail: kayacidev@gmail.com.

Transfers outside the EU. RevenueCat, Inc., Google LLC (ads), GitHub, Inc. (settings file) are based in the USA. Transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. Feedback messages may be processed outside the EU by Web3Forms and by the developer's e-mail provider (Google). Error reports from internal test builds are stored in Sentry's EU region.

Changes. If this policy changes, the date at the top is updated and the new version is shown in the game. Material changes, such as a new type of data or a new recipient, are announced in the game before they take effect.

Reklamy. Android od wersji kompilacji 82: reklama obowiązkowa jest rozważana tylko przy wejściu do nowego raportu miesięcznego, poza samouczkiem, po pierwszych 15 łącznych minutach na pierwszym planie zapisanych na urządzeniu i co najmniej 60 sekundach od powrotu. Reklama obowiązkowa może się pojawić dopiero co najmniej 10 minut po dowolnej wyświetlonej reklamie obowiązkowej lub z nagrodą; obejrzany film z nagrodą również uruchamia tę dziesięciominutową ochronę. Nie ma dodatkowego warunku dwóch raportów ani dziennego limitu liczby filmów. Dobrowolne filmy z nagrodą są dostępne po samouczku, gdy reklama jest gotowa, z przerwą co najmniej 90 sekund. Ukończony film dodaje pokazaną kwotę do kasy; nieudany zapis nagrody można ponowić bez kolejnego filmu. Starsze kompilacje Androida chronią pierwsze 15 aktywnych minut w sesji, rozważają reklamę przy zamykaniu raportu z odstępem dziesięciu minut i dwóch raportów oraz trzech minut po filmie z nagrodą; wspólna przerwa między filmami z nagrodą wynosi pięć minut. W każdej wersji jednorazowy zakup „Usuń reklamy” (także w „Pakiecie ulepszeń”) trwale usuwa reklamy obowiązkowe, a filmy z nagrodą pozostają dobrowolne. Nie ma banerów ani automatycznych propozycji wyświetlanych przy braku pieniędzy. Reklamy dostarcza Google AdMob (Google Ireland Ltd.). Wszyscy gracze otrzymują ograniczone, niespersonalizowane reklamy: gra ustawia znacznik Google dla osób poniżej wieku samodzielnego wyrażania zgody i przy każdym żądaniu wybiera reklamy niespersonalizowane, bez pytania o datę urodzenia. Ustawienia wyłączają personalizację i remarketing. Jeśli pozwalają na to uprawnienia platformy, Google Mobile Ads SDK może uzyskać dostęp do identyfikatora reklamowego Androida na potrzeby wyświetlania i pomiaru reklam oraz zapobiegania nadużyciom; gra żąda reklam niespersonalizowanych. Google może nadal przetwarzać adres IP, wynikającą z niego przybliżoną lokalizację, dane urządzenia i diagnostyczne oraz interakcje z reklamą, aby wyświetlać i mierzyć reklamy oraz zapobiegać nadużyciom. Przed pobraniem reklamy gra sprawdza wynik mechanizmu zgody Google; jeśli reklamy nie są dozwolone, nie pobiera ich. W tym ograniczonym trybie Google nie prosi o zgodę na reklamy spersonalizowane. Jeśli Google wymaga opcji prywatności, są one dostępne w Menu → O grze → Ustawienia prywatności reklam. Zob. https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Türkçe

Kebsik çevrimdışı çalışan bir oyundur. Oyun hesabı ve bağımsız oynanış analitiği yoktur. Reklam sağlayıcısının ölçümleri aşağıda açıklanmıştır. Oyun aylar arasında kısa tam ekran reklamlar gösterir (tek seferlik "Reklamları kaldır" alımıyla kaldırabilirsin); bir de senin izlemeyi seçtiğin isteğe bağlı ödüllü videolar vardır. Kebsik 13 yaşından küçük çocuklara yönelik değildir.

Cihazında kalanlar. Kayıt, dükkân adı, sahip adları ve ayarlar, açtığın satın almaların yerel bir kopyası ve son uygulama hatalarının kısa bir yerel kaydı (destek için). Oyunun kendisi bu verileri hiçbir yere göndermez. Oyunu silmek onları da siler. Cihazında Android yedeklemesi açıksa Android bu uygulama verilerini Google hesabındaki cihaz yedeğine ekleyebilir ve yeni telefonda geri yükleyebilir; geliştiricinin bu yedeklere erişimi yoktur, yedeklemeyi cihaz ayarlarından kapatabilirsin.

Kebsik ist ein Spiel, das offline funktioniert. Es gibt keine Spielkonten und keine unabhängige Spielanalyse. Die Messungen des Werbeanbieters sind unten beschrieben. Was auf deinem Gerät bleibt. Dein Spielstand, der Name des Ladens, die Namen der Inhaber und die Einstellungen, eine lokale Kopie deiner freigeschalteten Käufe sowie ein kurzes lokales Protokoll der letzten App-Fehler (für den Support).
