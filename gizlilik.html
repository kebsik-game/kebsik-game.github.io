Kebsik — Privacy Policy / Polityka prywatności / Gizlilik politikası / Datenschutzerklärung

Last updated: 2026-10-05

English

Kebsik is a game that works offline. It has no accounts and no analytics. It shows short full-screen ads between in-game months, which you can remove with the one-time "Remove ads" purchase, and optional rewarded videos you choose to watch. Kebsik is not directed at children under 16.

What stays on your device. Your save game, shop name, owner names and settings, a local copy of your unlocked purchases, and a short local log of the last app errors (for support). The game itself never sends this data anywhere. Uninstalling the game deletes it. If Android backup is turned on on your device, Android may include this app data in the device backup kept in your Google account and restore it on a new phone; the developer has no access to these backups, and you can turn backup off in your device settings.

Purchases. In-app purchases are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play and processed through RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA, revenuecat.com) to verify receipts and restore purchases. When the game starts it asks RevenueCat which items you own; this happens for every player, also if you have never bought anything. RevenueCat receives a pseudonymous app user ID (a random identifier created on your device; it is not your name, email or advertising ID), your purchase records and technical request data such as the IP address — not your name, email or card details. You can see this ID under Menu → About → Support ID. RevenueCat keeps the records for as long as they are needed to restore your purchases and for the developer's legal record-keeping duties, or until you ask for deletion. See https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

Ads. The game shows two kinds of ads. Short full-screen ads between months: only right after you close the monthly report, never in the first 6 in-game months, at most one every 15 minutes and every 3 monthly reports, never within 90 seconds after a sponsor video, at most 2 per play session and never while the tutorial is open; if an ad isn't ready, the game carries on without waiting. The one-time "Remove ads" purchase (also included in the "Upgrade bundle") removes them for good. Optional rewarded videos: on the monthly report, or from a small offer that sometimes appears when your shop is short of money (never in the first 2 in-game months, at most once every 10 minutes and 3 times an hour; it disappears by itself), you can choose to watch a short video ad in exchange for an in-game sponsor bonus (at most once per in-game month). No other ads are shown. Ads are served by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Ltd.), which may process your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, and ad interaction data to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. In the EU/EEA/UK the game asks for your consent before the first ad is requested — at the first ad break or when you tap the sponsor button, whichever comes first (Google's consent form); without consent only limited, non-personalised ads are shown or none at all. You can change your choice at any time under Menu → About → Ad privacy settings, or reset/delete the advertising ID in your device settings. See https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Game updates. In the Android and iOS app, each time the game starts it asks the update server of Expo (650 Industries, Inc., USA; expo.dev) whether a newer version of the game code is available, and downloads it if so. The request contains a random installation ID created by the update component on your device (not your advertising ID, not linked to your name or purchases), the app version, update channel and platform and, like any web request, your IP address. It is used only to deliver fixes and updates and to show the developer aggregate launch and error counts per update; it is not used for ads or tracking and not shared with anyone else. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data removes the ID. See https://expo.dev/privacy.

Game settings file. When the app starts it downloads a small public settings file from the developer's website (kebsik-game.github.io, hosted by GitHub, Inc.) that can adjust ad frequency or show a short notice. No personal data is sent; like any web request, GitHub receives your IP address and technical request data under its own privacy statement (https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

Error reports (internal test builds only). Only internal test builds that the developer installs on its own test devices send error reports to Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.); the versions on Google Play and the App Store and the web game never do. These reports contain technical JavaScript error messages and stack traces, the app version and platform; Sentry also sees the IP address used for delivery. No save games, shop or owner names, feedback messages, purchase records, screenshots, session recordings, device identifiers or native crash data are sent. See https://sentry.io/privacy/.

Feedback form (optional). Under Menu → About → Report a problem / suggestion you can write to the developer. Your name is optional; an e-mail address is enough for a reply. Only if you fill in the form and tick the consent box, your e-mail address, message, your name if you entered one and a few technical details (app version, device platform and system version, game language, currency and shop level; never your save game) are sent to the developer, either through the e-mail forwarding service Web3Forms (web3forms.com, see https://web3forms.com/privacy), which passes the message on to the developer's mailbox, or through your own e-mail app. They are used only to answer you, are kept for up to 12 months and then deleted.

Permissions. The game asks for no access to photos, camera, microphone, location or contacts. The advertising ID is used only by Google AdMob, and only for the ads described above. The "Share" button on an event card only opens your device's own share menu; the game itself sends nothing.

Legal bases (GDPR Art. 6). Purchase checks and restoring purchases: performance of the purchase contract (Art. 6(1)(b)); for players who have not bought anything, our legitimate interest in reliably restoring purchases (Art. 6(1)(f)). Personalised ads: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)). Limited, non-personalised ads: our legitimate interest in financing a free game (Art. 6(1)(f)); where the law requires consent for storing or reading information on your device, only with your consent given in Google's form. Game updates, the game settings file and error reports in internal test builds: our legitimate interest in delivering fixes and running the game securely (Art. 6(1)(f)). Feedback messages: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)).

Your rights. Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  1. access your data and get a copy (Art. 15);
  2. have incorrect data corrected (Art. 16);
  3. have your data deleted (Art. 17);
  4. restrict processing (Art. 18);
  5. data portability (Art. 20);
  6. object at any time to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21);
  7. withdraw your consent at any time; this does not affect processing that took place before the withdrawal (Art. 7(3));
  8. lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — in Poland the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO, uodo.gov.pl), or the authority of the country where you live or work.

How to make a request (including deletion). E-mail kayacidev@gmail.com and include your Support ID from Menu → About → Support ID; without it your pseudonymous purchase records cannot be found. The developer answers within one month and, for a deletion request, deletes your customer record at RevenueCat and any feedback messages from you. Deleting the RevenueCat record does not remove your purchases from your store account; you can get them back later with "Restore purchases". Everything stored on your device is deleted when you uninstall the game. Ad data is controlled by Google: change your choice under Menu → About → Ad privacy settings and reset or delete the advertising ID in your device settings. The update installation ID is random and not linked to you; uninstalling the game removes it.

Children. Kebsik is not directed at children under 16, and its store listing is for players aged 16 and over. The game does not ask anyone for a name, e-mail or other personal data; only the optional feedback form asks for an e-mail address. If you believe a child under 16 has sent us personal data, write to kayacidev@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Contact. Data controller: Hüseyin Kayacı, e-mail: kayacidev@gmail.com.

Transfers outside the EU. RevenueCat, Inc., Google LLC (ads), 650 Industries, Inc. (Expo, game updates) and GitHub, Inc. (settings file) are based in the USA. Transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. Feedback messages may be processed outside the EU by Web3Forms and by the developer's e-mail provider (Google). Error reports from internal test builds are stored in Sentry's EU region.

Changes. If this policy changes, the date at the top is updated and the new version is shown in the game. Material changes, such as a new type of data or a new recipient, are announced in the game before they take effect.

Dobrowolne filmy z nagrodą: w miesięcznym raporcie albo w małej propozycji, która czasem pojawia się, gdy w lokalu brakuje pieniędzy (nigdy w pierwszych 2 miesiącach gry, najwyżej raz na 10 minut i 3 razy na godzinę; znika sama), możesz obejrzeć krótką reklamę wideo w zamian za bonus sponsora w grze (najwyżej raz na miesiąc gry). Wybór możesz zmienić w każdej chwili w Menu → O grze → Ustawienia prywatności reklam albo zresetować/usunąć identyfikator reklamowy w ustawieniach urządzenia. Türkçe

Kebsik çevrimdışı çalışan bir oyundur. Hesap ve analitik yoktur. Oyun aylar arasında kısa tam ekran reklamlar gösterir (tek seferlik "Reklamları kaldır" alımıyla kaldırabilirsin); bir de senin izlemeyi seçtiğin isteğe bağlı ödüllü videolar vardır. Kebsik 16 yaşından küçük çocuklara yönelik değildir.

Cihazında kalanlar. Kayıt, dükkân adı, sahip adları ve ayarlar, açtığın satın almaların yerel bir kopyası ve son uygulama hatalarının kısa bir yerel kaydı (destek için). Oyunun kendisi bu verileri hiçbir yere göndermez. Oyunu silmek onları da siler. Cihazında Android yedeklemesi açıksa Android bu uygulama verilerini Google hesabındaki cihaz yedeğine ekleyebilir ve yeni telefonda geri yükleyebilir; geliştiricinin bu yedeklere erişimi yoktur, yedeklemeyi cihaz ayarlarından kapatabilirsin.

Satın almalar. Uygulama içi satın almaları Apple App Store ya da Google Play yürütür; RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., ABD, revenuecat.com) makbuzları doğrular ve alımları geri yükler. Oyun açılırken hangi ürünlere sahip olduğunu RevenueCat'e sorar; bu, hiç satın alma yapmamış olsan da her oyuncuda olur. RevenueCat takma adlı bir uygulama kullanıcı kimliği (cihazında oluşturulan rastgele bir kimlik; adın, e-postan ya da reklam kimliğin değildir), alım kayıtlarını ve IP adresi gibi isteğin teknik bilgilerini alır — adını, e-postanı ya da kart bilgilerini almaz. Bu kimliği Menü → Hakkında → Destek kimliği'nde görebilirsin. RevenueCat kayıtları alımlarını geri yükleyebilmek ve geliştiricinin yasal kayıt tutma yükümlülükleri için gerektiği sürece ya da silinmesini isteyene kadar saklar. Bkz. https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

Reklamlar. Oyunda iki tür reklam vardır. Aylar arasında kısa tam ekran reklamlar: yalnızca aylık raporu kapattıktan hemen sonra, ilk 6 oyun ayında hiç, en fazla 15 dakikada ve 3 aylık raporda bir, sponsor videosundan sonraki 90 saniye içinde hiç, bir oyun oturumunda en fazla 2 kez ve oyun rehberi açıkken hiç gösterilmez; reklam hazır değilse oyun beklemeden devam eder. Tek seferlik "Reklamları kaldır" alımı ("Geliştirme paketi"nde de vardır) bunları tamamen kaldırır. İsteğe bağlı ödüllü videolar: aylık raporda ya da dükkânda para sıkışınca bazen beliren küçük bir teklifte (ilk 2 oyun ayında hiç, en fazla 10 dakikada bir ve saatte 3 kez; kendiliğinden kaybolur), oyun içi bir sponsor bonusu karşılığında (oyun ayında en fazla bir kez) kısa bir video reklam izlemeyi seçebilirsin. Başka reklam gösterilmez. Reklamları Google AdMob (Google Ireland Ltd.) sunar; reklamı göstermek, ölçmek ve dolandırıcılığı önlemek için cihazının reklam kimliğini, IP adresini, bundan çıkan yaklaşık konumu ve reklam etkileşim verilerini işleyebilir. AB/AEA/Birleşik Krallık'ta oyun, ilk reklam istenmeden önce onayını sorar — ilk reklam arasında ya da sponsor düğmesine dokunduğunda, hangisi önce gelirse (Google'ın onay formu); onay yoksa yalnızca sınırlı, kişiselleştirilmemiş reklam gösterilir ya da hiç gösterilmez. Seçimini istediğin zaman Menü → Hakkında → Reklam gizlilik ayarları'ndan değiştirebilir, reklam kimliğini cihaz ayarlarından sıfırlayabilir ya da silebilirsin. Bkz. https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Deutsch

Kebsik ist ein Spiel, das offline funktioniert. Es gibt keine Konten und keine Analyse. Zwischen den Spielmonaten zeigt es kurze Vollbild-Werbung, die du mit dem einmaligen Kauf „Werbung entfernen“ abschalten kannst, sowie freiwillige Belohnungsvideos, die du selbst auswählst. Kebsik richtet sich nicht an Kinder unter 16 Jahren.

Was auf deinem Gerät bleibt. Dein Spielstand, der Name des Ladens, die Namen der Inhaber und die Einstellungen, eine lokale Kopie deiner freigeschalteten Käufe sowie ein kurzes lokales Protokoll der letzten App-Fehler (für den Support). Das Spiel selbst sendet diese Daten nirgendwohin. Beim Deinstallieren des Spiels werden sie gelöscht. Ist auf deinem Gerät die Android-Sicherung aktiviert, kann Android diese App-Daten in die Gerätesicherung in deinem Google-Konto aufnehmen und auf einem neuen Telefon wiederherstellen; der Entwickler hat keinen Zugriff auf diese Sicherungen, und du kannst die Sicherung in den Geräteeinstellungen ausschalten.

Käufe. In-App-Käufe werden über den Apple App Store oder Google Play abgewickelt und über RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA, revenuecat.com) verarbeitet, um Belege zu prüfen und Käufe wiederherzustellen. Beim Start fragt das Spiel RevenueCat, welche Artikel du besitzt; das geschieht bei allen Spielern, auch wenn du nie etwas gekauft hast. RevenueCat erhält eine pseudonyme App-Nutzer-ID (eine auf deinem Gerät erzeugte Zufallskennung; nicht dein Name, deine E-Mail oder deine Werbe-ID), deine Kaufdaten und technische Anfragedaten wie die IP-Adresse — nicht deinen Namen, deine E-Mail oder deine Kartendaten. Diese ID siehst du unter Menü → Über → Support-ID. RevenueCat speichert die Daten, solange sie zum Wiederherstellen deiner Käufe und für die gesetzlichen Aufbewahrungspflichten des Entwicklers nötig sind, oder bis du die Löschung verlangst. Siehe https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

Werbung. Das Spiel zeigt zwei Arten von Werbung. Kurze Vollbild-Werbung zwischen den Monaten: nur direkt nachdem du den Monatsbericht geschlossen hast, nie in den ersten 6 Spielmonaten, höchstens einmal alle 15 Minuten und alle 3 Monatsberichte, nie innerhalb von 90 Sekunden nach einem Sponsorvideo, höchstens 2-mal pro Spielsitzung und nie, solange die Anleitung geöffnet ist; ist keine Werbung bereit, läuft das Spiel ohne Warten weiter. Der einmalige Kauf „Werbung entfernen“ (auch im „Upgrade-Paket“ enthalten) entfernt sie dauerhaft. Freiwillige Belohnungsvideos: Im Monatsbericht oder in einem kleinen Angebot, das manchmal erscheint, wenn im Laden das Geld knapp ist (nie in den ersten 2 Spielmonaten, höchstens alle 10 Minuten und 3-mal pro Stunde; es verschwindet von selbst), kannst du dir freiwillig ein kurzes Werbevideo ansehen und bekommst dafür einen Sponsorbonus im Spiel (höchstens einmal pro Spielmonat). Andere Werbung gibt es nicht. Die Werbung liefert Google AdMob (Google Ireland Ltd.); dabei können die Werbe-ID deines Geräts, deine IP-Adresse, ein daraus abgeleiteter ungefährer Standort und Daten zur Interaktion mit der Werbung verarbeitet werden, um Werbung auszuliefern, zu messen und Betrug zu verhindern. In der EU/im EWR/im Vereinigten Königreich fragt das Spiel vor der ersten Werbeanfrage nach deiner Einwilligung — bei der ersten Werbepause oder wenn du auf den Sponsor-Button tippst, je nachdem, was zuerst kommt (Einwilligungsformular von Google); ohne Einwilligung wird nur eingeschränkte, nicht personalisierte Werbung oder gar keine gezeigt. Du kannst deine Wahl jederzeit unter Menü → Über → Datenschutz-Einstellungen für Werbung ändern oder die Werbe-ID in den Geräteeinstellungen zurücksetzen bzw. löschen. Siehe https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.
